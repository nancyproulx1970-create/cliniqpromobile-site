What should I charge for a home visit? Free calculator

On home visits, your time doesn't stop at the patient's door: there's the drive, and a car that costs money to run. This calculator adds it all up, using your own numbers.

Short answer: a home visit price that covers your time and your travel is (length of care + round-trip travel time) × your target hourly rate, plus your kilometres × your cost per kilometre. The free calculator below does the math. It suggests no fee: you set your own prices.

Free calculator

Every number comes from you: the tool suggests no fee. Nothing is sent: the calculation happens in your browser.

The math, spelled out

(length of care + round-trip travel time) × target hourly rate + kilometres × cost per kilometre

Driving time is working time: the tool counts it at the same hourly rate as the care. If you see several patients on the same run, enter only the share of the drive that belongs to this visit.

Finding your cost per kilometre

Your cost per kilometre is your own: add up your vehicle expenses for the year (fuel or charging, maintenance, insurance, registration, interest or lease) and divide by your total kilometres for the year, from the odometer. The 73¢/km rate you may have heard about is the limit an employer can pay an employee tax-free in 2026: it is not your cost. The mileage deduction guide.

What about tax?

The result is a price before tax. Most health care is exempt; some services are taxable. Try the "Which tax on my services?" tool.

Frequently asked questions

How do I work out a price for a home visit?

Add the length of care and the round-trip travel time, multiply by your target hourly rate, then add your kilometres multiplied by your cost per kilometre.

Which cost per kilometre should I use?

Your own: your vehicle expenses for the year divided by your total kilometres for the year. The 73¢/km rate is the limit an employer can pay an employee tax-free in 2026; it is not your cost.

Does the tool suggest a fee?

No. Every number comes from you. The tool only shows the math, line by line. You set your own prices.

Are my numbers sent anywhere?

No. The calculation happens in your browser and nothing is stored.
