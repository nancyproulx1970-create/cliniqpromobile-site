1. CliniQ Pro: the best choice for self-employed professionals

CliniQ Pro is built for one person working on their own, whether they see patients in a clinic, travel to their homes, or both. Each work location has its own schedule: at the clinic, no mileage is counted, since the patient comes to you; for home visits, business mileage is calculated between visits. “Find a time slot” suggests a time in the right place.

Everything included for $44.95 a month:

  • Schedule: hours by work location, “Find a time slot”, directions in one tap, text reminders 24 hours before, online booking by location (clinic, long-term care home, residence) with a QR code, statutory holidays shown.
  • Patient record: health assessment, history, medications, change history, dated consents, before/after photos, documents received, access log, and a copy of the complete record (identity, health assessment and all finalized notes) to print or share, logged in the register of copies released.
  • Notes: templates that fit your practice, a writing assistant, notes signed then locked, late notes dated as such.
  • Billing: PDF receipts ready for insurance, with your regulatory college, licence number and your province's taxes.
  • Expenses and accounting: photo of the receipt, mileage calculated from your schedule, odometer photo, vehicle business-use share, tax report (GST/HST, QST) and a year-end summary for your accountant, following the lines of your tax return.
  • Sterilization: cycle log and traceability of sterilized instruments.
  • And more: patient list import and export, core features even without a connection, data hosted in Canada (Montreal), two-step sign-in, English and French, created by a licensed practical nurse.
  • Limits: launches January 1, 2027; no direct billing to provincial plans; direct billing to insurers coming soon (for now, the patient sends the receipt); no shared records between colleagues.

2. Jane: for a clinic with several practitioners

Jane is well-known and complete, with very polished online booking. The Balance plan, at CA$59 a month, includes 20 appointments a month, and several features are add-ons. For home visits, mileage is often tracked in another app. See CliniQ Pro vs Jane.

3. Cliniko: for a growing allied health clinic

Cliniko includes every feature on every plan; only the number of practitioners changes the price: US$45 a month for one person, US$95 for 2 to 5. Text reminders are paid separately, US$0.10 per message. Prices are shown in US dollars.

4. ClinicSense: for massage therapy and marketing

ClinicSense targets therapists (massage therapy, physiotherapy, chiropractic) who also want marketing tools. The Lite plan, at US$39 a month, suits fewer than 20 appointments a month; text reminders and review and newsletter tools are on higher plans.

What about mileage apps (MileIQ, Everlance)?

A common approach is to use clinic software and add a mileage app such as MileIQ, Driversnote or Everlance (Starter plan at US$8.99 a month). It works, but it means two subscriptions and trips to sort by hand, since the app doesn't know which trip was an appointment. In CliniQ Pro, mileage is calculated from your schedule: each trip is already linked to an appointment. See automatic mileage.

Do you bill a provincial health plan?

If you are a physician or a nurse practitioner who bills a provincial plan, look for a certified electronic medical record in your province instead. CliniQ Pro and most software on this page are built for practices where the patient pays directly.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best software for a self-employed health professional in Canada?

For one person, at home, in a clinic or both, CliniQ Pro brings together scheduling, records, signed notes, receipts, expenses and mileage for $44.95 a month all included, with data in Canada, in English and French. For a clinic with several practitioners, Jane or Cliniko are a better fit. This page is published by CliniQ Pro.

Which software should I choose if I work at home and in a clinic?

Software that handles your schedule by location. In CliniQ Pro, each work location has its own schedule: clinic days count no mileage, and home visits calculate business mileage between visits. The two never get mixed up.

How much does Cliniko cost in Canada?

According to Cliniko's website, US$45 a month for one practitioner and US$95 for 2 to 5 practitioners, with every feature included. Text messages are paid separately, US$0.10 per message. The price in Canadian dollars varies with the exchange rate.

How much does ClinicSense cost?

ClinicSense's Lite plan costs US$39 a month and suits fewer than 20 appointments a month. The Standard and Premium plans cost more and add text reminders and marketing tools.

Do I need a separate mileage app like MileIQ or Everlance?

Not with CliniQ Pro: mileage is calculated from your schedule, since each trip is linked to an appointment. With clinic software that has no mileage, you need a separate app such as MileIQ or Everlance, and you have to sort your trips.

Which software keeps data in Canada?

CliniQ Pro hosts records in Canada, in Montreal. For other software, ask the vendor where data is hosted: it matters under Canadian privacy laws and professional confidentiality.
