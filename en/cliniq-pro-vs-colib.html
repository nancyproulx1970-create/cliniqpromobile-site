CliniQ Pro vs Colib: which software for self-employed health professionals?

Your whole practice in one app. One tap, and your accountant has the summary.

CliniQ Pro is software and an app built for self-employed health professionals of every profession across Canada, at home, in a clinic or both, with mileage and expenses included. When Colib may suit you better

If you see few clients a month (fewer than 15), work mostly in an office or by telehealth, or want online booking, built-in video sessions or a clinic with several practitioners, Colib offers these features.

When CliniQ Pro is made for you

You are self-employed and you travel: nurse, practical nurse, foot care, wound care, physiotherapy, occupational therapy, massage therapy or other. You want one price, without counting appointments, with mileage, expenses and your accountant's summary in the same app, on your phone.

Frequently asked questions

Is CliniQ Pro cheaper than Colib?

It depends on your volume. Colib charges $3 per appointment ($9 monthly minimum) or $75 a month for its Practitioner plan. CliniQ Pro costs CA$44.95 a month with unlimited appointments. From 15 appointments a month, CliniQ Pro costs less (prices checked November 16, 2026).

Does Colib track mileage?

Colib's website mentions neither mileage nor expense tracking (checked November 16, 2026). CliniQ Pro calculates mileage from your schedule and files your expenses with a photo of the receipt.

Does Colib have a mobile app?

According to its own FAQ, Colib is web-based software. CliniQ Pro is an app installed on Android and iPhone, designed to be used between visits.

Does CliniQ Pro protect privacy like Colib?

Both host data in Canada. CliniQ Pro is designed to help you meet Quebec's Law 25 and PIPEDA: access limited to your own records, signed notes that can't be changed, dated consents and an access log. Compliance also depends on your own practices.
