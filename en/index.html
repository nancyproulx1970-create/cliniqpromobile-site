A day with CliniQ Pro

From morning coffee to coming home.

  1. 7:45 am
    7:45 am

    Coffee first, then the home screen.

    The home screen sticks to the essentials: what's left to complete, whether something slipped through the cracks, and today's appointments.

    Your patients got their reminder by text the day before, on its own, 24 hours ahead. And your phone reminds you of your first appointment 1 hour before.

    A patient calls? “Trouver un créneau” (Find a time slot) suggests open times based on the drive from your previous visit, so you don't zigzag from one town to another.

  2. 8:10 am
    8:10 am

    One tap, and the GPS is on its way.

    Tap the appointment's directions icon: the route to the patient's home opens in your phone's GPS. No more copying addresses in the car.

  3. 8:30 am
    8:30 am

    At your first patient's home, hands full.

    Big buttons, few steps: everything works with one thumb, even with gloves on.

    Signed, locked note

    Once finalized, the note no longer changes. Forgot something? A dated late entry is added next to it.

    Every sterile pouch traced

    Tap the pouch you used. If a batch is recalled, you know right away which patients are affected.

    Photos and consents

    Photos sorted by side and by moment, before or after care. Consent can also be given by text message. The register keeps the consent, refusal or withdrawal, and who gave it: the patient or their representative.

  4. 9:20 am
    9:20 am

    The receipt is sent before your coat is back on.

    Invoices numbered with no gaps. The PDF receipt can be printed or sent to the patient by email or text message, with the date of their next appointment. Payment declined or wrong method? “Correct the payment”, in one tap. Your income is already counted.

  5. 12:05 pm
    12:05 pm

    Filling up on gas? Snap a photo.

    Photograph the receipt: the amount is read for you, a quick look to confirm, and the expense is filed with its supporting document. A bill that's also for home use, like your cell phone? You enter your business-use share once, and it's calculated on every bill.

  6. 5:40 pm
    5:40 pm

    In the driveway, your mileage is already done.

    The trips between your visits are calculated for you: a quick look to confirm the day, and your mileage log is up to date. The odometer is one photo of the dashboard once a month, not every trip. No more logbook in the glove box.

  7. 6:15 pm
    6:15 pm

    The sterilizer is done, and so is the log.

    Record your cycle once it's finished: time, indicators read, numbered pouches. The pouch codes are scanned with the camera. Your spore tests have their place in the log. The day someone asks for it, your sterilization log is complete and up to date.

  8. Dec. 31
    December 31

    “Envoyer à mon comptable” (Send to my accountant)

    A single button prepares your annual summary as a PDF: your income, your expenses sorted by the lines of the income tax return, the business share of your shared expenses, your mileage and your vehicle's business-use share. Your expense receipts come along, with their photos. All that's left is to send it to your accountant.

Your patients' records, protected at every step.

You remain responsible for your records. CliniQ Pro was built from the start to help you protect them.

Hosted in Canada

Records are hosted in Montréal. A few features rely on services outside Canada (reading receipts and the odometer, calculating trips, help, error reporting, sign-in with Google): the details will be in the privacy policy.

Encrypted

In transit. Encryption on the server is handled by the hosting provider.

Your patients stay yours

The database itself prevents one account from seeing another account's patients.

Phone locked

Automatic lock, password or fingerprint. Screenshots blocked by default (you can allow them in the settings), and no copy in the phone's backup.

Password protected

Forgot your password? A 6-digit code by email, then every session is closed, on every device.

Two-step verification

Optional: a code from your authenticator app at each new sign-in. A stolen password is no longer enough.

Safeguards against mistakes

Locked notes, receipts that can't be changed, protected photos. A log keeps the important actions, and consents, copies released and incidents each have their own register.

Frequently asked questions

In the meantime, here are the questions I'm asked most often.

Before you start

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. CliniQ Pro will be available at the same time on Android and iPhone, on phones and tablets.

Do I need Internet access?

For most features, yes: your phone's cellular data is enough. Without a signal, you're not stuck: the app opens, appointments, notes and photos you've already viewed stay visible, and a note, a care photo or an expense receipt is kept on the phone, then sent on its own when the signal returns.

Can I use it on a tablet or a second phone?

Yes, with the same account, on a phone or a tablet.

If I stop, what happens to my data?

You can export your patient list and your expense receipts at any time. The rules for record retention and account closure will be set out in the terms of use.

Is it right for my profession?

Yes. CliniQ Pro is made for all home health care.

My patients come to my clinic. Is CliniQ Pro right for me?

Yes. You choose in your profile: at your patients’ homes, at your clinic, or both. You keep every tool (schedule, records, notes, receipts, expenses, sterilization, accountant summary). Only mileage isn’t used for patients who come to you.

Getting started

How do I bring in my current patients?

You can import them all at once, from a CSV file taken from Excel or from the tool you use now. You can also add them one by one.

Do my patients need an account?

No. Only the professional has an account. Your patients have nothing to install.

Is there help if I get stuck?

Yes. The app contains more than 120 answers, sorted by topic, with a search bar. An automated assistant (AI) also answers questions about using the app; never write any patient information in it.

Scheduling and the road

Does the app calculate my mileage?

Yes. The trips between your visits are calculated for you, and you confirm the day at a glance. You photograph the odometer once a month.

My patient isn't home when I arrive. Is my trip lost?

No. You check “Patient absent” and the trip still counts, even without care.

Do my patients get a reminder?

Yes. A text message goes out on its own 24 hours before each appointment. One switch in Settings, nothing else to set up.

Does the app help me fit in a new appointment?

Yes. “Trouver un créneau” (Find a time slot) suggests open times based on the town and the length of the visit. The app also warns you if the travel time between two appointments looks too short.

Notes and records

Can I change a note once it's signed?

No, and that's on purpose. A finalized note is locked under your name, with the date and time. If you forgot something, you add a dated late entry next to it.

Can I file a prescription or a nursing care plan (PTI) I received?

Yes. A PDF or a photo of the document is filed in the patient's record, and the original stays intact.

Is there a sterilization log?

Yes. You record your cycles, your numbered pouches and your spore tests. Each pouch used is linked to the visit where it was used.

Money and your accountant

Can I bill for a product, like a cream?

Yes. You add it to the invoice after the care.

Does the app read my expense receipts?

Yes, from a photo: the date, the total and the merchant are filled in for you. You check before saving.

I also use my cell phone at home. How is that counted?

You enter your business-use share, as a percentage, once a year for the category: it applies to all your bills. For a specific bill, you can enter the business amount instead, with a note. Confirm the right percentage with your accountant.

What does my accountant receive?

A one-page PDF with the year's totals: income, expenses by category and vehicle, following the lines of the income tax return, and each tax on its own line (GST, QST, HST, PST), collected and paid. No patient names appear on it. Your accountant always confirms the classification.

What if I'm not registered for sales taxes?

The app takes that into account: no tax is added to your invoices. It warns you when your taxable sales get close to the threshold where registration becomes mandatory.

Security

What happens if I lose my phone?

The app locks itself and asks for your password to reopen. From another device, you can change your password and sign out your other devices.
