Clinic management software and app for solo health professionals

Do you see patients at your clinic, your office or your home, and sometimes at theirs too? CliniQ Pro is practice management software and an EMR app built for solo health practitioners across Canada: patient records, scheduling and billing run on your phone or tablet, with no computer and nothing to install.

At the clinic, at home, or both

In your profile, choose “My patients come to me”, “At my patients' homes” or “Both”. With “Both”, each patient is marked “Seen at the clinic” or “Seen at home”, and the app uses it for your schedule and your mileage.

Simple scheduling for your clinic

Your services with their price and duration, appointments in a few taps, and automatic text reminders 24 hours ahead, sent only to patients who agreed.

The complete patient record

Health assessment, signed and locked clinical notes, before/after care photos, dated consents and documents received. Your patient list imports from an Excel or CSV file taken from your old software.

Receipts, invoices and bookkeeping

Numbered invoice and PDF receipt handed over on the spot, by email or by text, with taxes calculated for your province. Expenses are sorted from a photo of the receipt, and a yearly summary is ready for your accountant.

Sterilization log

Every cycle, every spore test and every pouch used for a patient is recorded: your instrument traceability is ready if your professional order asks for it.

Who is it for?

Nurses and licensed practical nurses, foot care, wound care, physiotherapy, occupational therapy, respiratory therapy, mental health, dental care, medical aesthetics and any other independent health practice, across Canada, in English and French.

Frequently asked questions

Is it software or an app?

Both: CliniQ Pro is a mobile app for Android and iPhone (phone and tablet) that does the job of clinic management software, with no computer and nothing to install.

I see patients at my clinic and also at home. Is that possible?

Yes. Choose “Both” in your profile: each patient is marked “Seen at the clinic” or “Seen at home”. Trips between your home and your clinic don't count as business mileage, under the CRA rule.

My clinic is in my home. How do I set that up?

In your profile, check “My clinic is at my address”.

Can I bring over the patient list from my old software?

Yes. Export it as an Excel or CSV file, then import it into CliniQ Pro: the app checks the file before adding anything.
