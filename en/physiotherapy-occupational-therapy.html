Home and clinic: how to choose

Many physiotherapists and occupational therapists do both: some days at the clinic, others in patients' homes. Before choosing software, ask yourself three questions.

  • Does mileage stay separate? In CliniQ Pro, each work location has its own schedule. At the clinic, the patient comes to you, so no mileage is counted. For home visits, business mileage is calculated between visits. With many clinic systems, mileage is kept in a separate app: check before you choose.
  • Does the schedule account for the road? “Find a time slot” suggests a time in the right place: at the clinic, or in the right area for a home visit.
  • Is everything in one subscription? In CliniQ Pro, records, signed notes, receipts, expenses and mileage are included. Clinic-first software such as Jane App is strong for online booking and insurance billing. Compare based on how you work.

Frequently asked questions

I work at home and in a clinic: will my mileage get mixed up?

No. In CliniQ Pro, each work location has its own schedule. Clinic days count no mileage, since the patient comes to you. Home visits calculate business mileage between visits. The two stay separate.

Is it suitable for my profession?

Yes. CliniQ Pro is made for all home health care, including physiotherapy, occupational therapy and rehabilitation. Your profile states your profession and your regulatory college.

Can I use CliniQ Pro outside Quebec?

Yes. CliniQ Pro works across Canada, in English and in French. Taxes are added based on your province.

Is CliniQ Pro a physiotherapy EMR?

Yes. CliniQ Pro works as a mobile EMR for self-employed physiotherapists and occupational therapists in Canada: patient records, signed treatment notes, dated consents, scheduling, receipts for insurance and mileage, at the clinic or at home.

Is CliniQ Pro made for physiotherapists in private practice?

Yes. CliniQ Pro is made for self-employed physiotherapists, physiotherapy technologists and occupational therapists in private practice, in a private clinic or at home.
