GST/HST and QST for self-employed health professionals (2026)

Short answer: most health care given by a member of a recognized profession is exempt from GST/HST (and QST in Quebec): you do not charge tax on that care. You must register only if your taxable sales (products sold, cosmetic services, etc.) exceed $30,000.

Which care is exempt?

Health care rendered to an individual, within the scope of the profession, by a professional who holds a licence (or, where the profession is not regulated, equivalent qualifications):

  • nurses, including practical nurses (RPN/LPN), in a nurse-patient relationship;
  • physiotherapists, occupational therapists, speech-language pathologists, audiologists;
  • psychologists, social workers;
  • dietitians, podiatrists, chiropractors, optometrists;
  • midwives, dental hygienists.

Since June 20, 2024, psychotherapy and counselling therapy by a qualified practitioner are also exempt.

The title alone does not decide: the service must be for the protection, maintenance or restoration of health. A report or assessment done for an insurer, a lawyer or an employer can be taxable, even when done by an exempt profession.

What generally stays taxable

  • selling products (creams, accessories, etc.);
  • cosmetic services with no medical purpose;
  • kinesiology;
  • care given by someone outside a recognized profession (for example, foot care given without a nursing or podiatry licence).

New: massage therapy

The November 5, 2026 federal budget proposes to exempt from GST/HST the services of massage therapists who hold the licence required in their province or, where none is required, equivalent qualifications, for services rendered after November 5, 2026. Quebec announced it will harmonize the QST once the federal law is passed. This is a proposed measure: until the law is passed, confirm how to bill with your accountant or the CRA.

The $30,000 threshold (small supplier)

You are a "small supplier" as long as your taxable sales do not exceed $30,000 in a single quarter or over the last four quarters. Your exempt care does not count in that total. It is a total of sales, not profit. If you go over the threshold in a single quarter, you must collect tax starting with the sale that puts you over.

If you only provide exempt care, you do not register, and you do not recover the tax paid on your purchases (phone, equipment, software). If you also have taxable sales and are registered, you recover only the share of tax related to those sales.

Frequently asked questions

Does a self-employed nurse or physiotherapist charge GST/HST?

No, not on exempt health care rendered to a patient within their profession. Products sold and cosmetic services can be taxable.

When do I have to register for GST/HST?

When your taxable sales exceed $30,000 in a single quarter or over the last four quarters. Exempt care does not count in that total.

Is massage therapy still taxable?

The November 5, 2026 federal budget proposes to exempt it for massage therapists who hold their province's licence (or equivalent qualifications), for services rendered after November 5, 2026. It is a proposed measure: confirm with your accountant until the law is passed.

What is the HST rate in Nova Scotia in 2026?

14%, since April 1, 2025.
