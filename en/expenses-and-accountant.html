Expense tracking and a year-end summary for your accountant

Your expenses sorted, your accountant served.

Gas, supplies, cell phone: each expense is filed with its receipt the moment you make it.

A photo of the receipt, and it is filed

From a photo, the date, the total and the merchant are filled in for you. You check before saving, and the photo stays attached to the expense.

Expenses shared with your home

For a bill that is also used at home, like your cell phone, you enter your business share, as a percentage, once a year for the category. For a specific bill, you can enter the business amount instead, with a note.

“Send to my accountant”

One button prepares your year-end PDF summary: income, expenses sorted by income tax return line, the business share of shared expenses, mileage and vehicle business use. Your receipts follow, with their photos. No patient names appear.

The app is available in English and French.

Sales tax report: monthly, quarterly or yearly

If you are registered for sales tax, CliniQ Pro prepares your sales tax report for your filing frequency: every month, every quarter or once a year. It shows the tax collected on your taxable sales, the input tax credits on your expenses and the amount to remit, laid out like your GST/HST return (and QST in Quebec). Exempt health services are counted separately. One button produces the report as a PDF.

Not registered yet? The app tracks your taxable sales over the last four quarters and warns you as you approach the $30,000 threshold.

Based on your province

The accountant summary and your receipts adapt to the province in your profile:

  • Quebec: federal T2125 lines, with the matching TP-80 line, and GST + QST.
  • Ontario, New Brunswick, Nova Scotia, Prince Edward Island, Newfoundland and Labrador: T2125 and HST.
  • British Columbia, Saskatchewan, Manitoba: T2125, GST and PST, each in its own box.
  • Alberta and the territories: T2125 and GST.

Your accountant always confirms the classification.

Frequently asked questions

Does the app read my expense receipts?

Yes, from a photo: the date, the total and the merchant are filled in for you. You check before saving.

Is there a quarterly sales tax report?

Yes. If you are registered for sales tax, pick your frequency (month, quarter or year): your GST/HST report, and QST in Quebec, is prepared for you with the amount to remit, as a PDF.

What does my accountant receive?

A one-page PDF with the year's totals: income, expenses by category and vehicle, by income tax return line. No patient names appear. Your accountant always confirms the classification.
