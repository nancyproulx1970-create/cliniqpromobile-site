Insurance receipts and invoices for self-employed health practitioners

Short answer: for a client to be reimbursed by their insurer, your receipt should show the name of the person who received care, the date and type of each treatment, the amount paid, your name, your college or association and your registration or membership number. Sales tax appears only if your services are taxable and you are registered for GST/HST.

What insurers ask for on a receipt

Insurers' claim forms for paramedical care ask for:

  • the patient's name (the person who received the care);
  • the date of each treatment;
  • the type of treatment and the fee for each one;
  • the practitioner's name;
  • their college or association, and their registration or membership number.

Also add your contact details, the payment method and a note that the amount was paid.

Mistakes to avoid

  • A receipt in the name of someone other than the person treated: insurers treat this as fraud.
  • A service described as something other than what was done.
  • A receipt with no registration or membership number: it is often refused.

Self-employed invoices: with or without tax

Your care is exempt (nurses, physiotherapists, occupational therapists and other professions listed in the law): no tax on the receipt, even if you are registered.

Your services are taxable and you earn under $30,000 in taxable sales a year: you are a small supplier. You do not have to register, and your invoice shows no tax and no tax number.

Your services are taxable and you are registered: your invoice shows GST/HST (and QST in Quebec) separately, with your registration numbers. See our tax guide.

Frequently asked questions

What should a receipt for insurance include?

The name of the person treated, the date and type of each treatment, the amount, your name, your college or association and your registration or membership number.

Does a self-employed practitioner put tax on their invoice?

Only if their services are taxable and they are registered. Under $30,000 in taxable sales a year, they don't have to register. Exempt health care never has tax.

How long should receipts be kept?

Insurers can ask for receipts after payment: Sun Life, for example, asks its clients to keep them for at least a year. On your side, keep your copies as long as your records.
