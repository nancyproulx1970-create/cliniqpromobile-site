Do I need to register for GST/HST? $30,000 threshold calculator

Are you a self-employed health professional who also sells products or taxable services? See in a minute where you stand against the registration threshold.

Short answer: you must register for GST/HST (and QST in Quebec) once your taxable sales go over $30,000 in a single calendar quarter or over four calendar quarters in a row. Your exempt health care does not count. The free calculator below does the math and tells you when tax starts.

How the threshold works

You are a "small supplier" as long as your taxable sales stay at or under $30,000 in a single calendar quarter and over four calendar quarters in a row. It is a total of sales before expenses, not profit.

Your situationWhat happens
$30,000 or less over four quartersYou are a small supplier. You don't have to register, but you can choose to.
More than $30,000 in a single quarterYou stop being a small supplier with the sale that puts you over. You charge tax starting with that sale.
More than $30,000 over four quarters, but not in a single oneYou remain a small supplier until the end of the month after the quarter in which you went over. You charge tax starting with your first taxable sale after that date.

In both cases, you have 29 days to register from the day tax starts to apply. In Quebec, you register for both the GST and the QST with Revenu Québec.

What counts in the total

Counted: your taxable sales in Canada and abroad, including zero-rated sales, from all your businesses and your associates. Not counted: your exempt health care, the sale of capital property (your equipment, for example) and goodwill.

If you only provide exempt care, you do not register, and you do not recover the tax paid on your purchases. To find out whether your service is exempt or taxable, try the "Which tax on my services?" tool and read the GST, HST and QST guide.

Frequently asked questions

When do I have to register for GST/HST?

When your taxable sales go over $30,000 in a single calendar quarter or over four calendar quarters in a row. Exempt health care does not count in that total.

Does my exempt health care count toward the $30,000?

No. Only taxable sales count: products sold, cosmetic services and other taxable services. If you only provide exempt care, you do not register.

What happens if I go over $30,000 in a single quarter?

You stop being a small supplier with the sale that puts you over the threshold. You charge tax starting with that sale, and you have 29 days to register.

What if I go over across four quarters, but not in a single one?

You remain a small supplier until the end of the month after the quarter in which you went over. You charge tax starting with your first taxable sale after that date, and you have 29 days to register.
