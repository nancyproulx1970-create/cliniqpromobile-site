Short answer: in Canada, the main Jane App alternatives for a self-employed health practice are CliniQ Pro, GOrendezvous, Owl Practice, Noterro, Colib, Carepatron, Psylio, Medexa, ShiftCare and Cliniciel. Why look for a Jane alternative

Jane is well-known, complete software. Common reasons to look elsewhere: a starting plan limited by number of appointments, features sold as add-ons, and needs specific to home care, such as travel and mileage.

Jane plus a mileage app, or one tool?

Many health professionals who do home visits pair practice software, such as Jane, with a mileage app, such as MileIQ or Driversnote, and sometimes a spreadsheet for expenses. That means two or three subscriptions and trips to sort by hand.

CliniQ Pro brings it all into one app: mileage is calculated from your schedule, since the purpose of each trip is already known from the appointment. Expenses, the tax report and the yearly summary for your accountant are in the same place.

CliniQ Pro is built in Canada, for Canada: receipts and the tax report follow your province's rules (HST in Ontario and the Atlantic provinces, GST elsewhere, GST and QST in Quebec), while international software like Carepatron bills in US dollars and is not built around Canadian sales taxes.

How to choose

  • Work out the total price for a normal month, add-ons included.
  • Check that your data is hosted in Canada and that the software helps you meet PIPEDA and, in Quebec, Law 25.
  • Check that the app and support are offered in your language, English or French.
  • Check that signed notes can no longer be changed.
  • Check what you'll hand your accountant: some software stops at receipts and leaves you to compile expenses elsewhere.
  • If you do home visits, check how travel and mileage are handled.
  • Try the app in your language, on your phone.
