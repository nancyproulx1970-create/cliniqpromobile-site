CliniQ Pro vs Yocale: which app for self-employed health professionals?

Your practice. Your business. One tool. One tap, and your accountant has the summary.

See also our guide: how to choose software in Canada (2026)

Short answer: Yocale is booking and payment software built for salons, spas, med spas and clinics. Its clinical notes come with the “Clinical” plans. CliniQ Pro is software and an app built for the self-employed health professional anywhere in Canada, at home, in a clinic or both. For one person with clinical notes: CA$44.95 a month with CliniQ Pro, US$99 a month with Yocale.

Three things to check during your trial, whatever the software. Can you export your records if you switch apps? Can you add photos to your notes? Does the app work with no signal at a client's home? In CliniQ Pro: your client list exports as a CSV file and a client's complete record can be shared or printed; before-and-after photos are taken during the visit; and with no signal, your photos and receipts wait in the app and are sent automatically when the signal comes back.

With CliniQ Pro, taxes are added based on your province. Yearly plan: CA$449.50 (two months free). 14-day free trial, no credit card needed.

Included in CliniQ Pro, at the same price

Scheduling by location and routes, text reminders, patient records, signed and locked notes, before-and-after photos, consents, PDF receipts and invoices, sterilization log, expenses and accountant summary, automatic mileage. Data hosted in Canada. In English and French, on Android and iPhone.

When Yocale may suit you better

If you run a salon, spa or med spa with a team, if your clients need to book and pay online themselves, or if you want a built-in card terminal, Yocale offers these features.

When CliniQ Pro is made for you

You are self-employed, at home, in a clinic or both: foot care, nursing, wound care, physiotherapy, occupational therapy, massage therapy or other. You want one price in Canadian dollars, everything included, with mileage, expenses, the sterilization log and your accountant's summary in the same app.

Frequently asked questions

Is CliniQ Pro cheaper than Yocale?

Yes, for one person who needs clinical notes: CA$44.95 a month with CliniQ Pro, everything included, versus US$99 a month for Yocale Clinical Pro (US$84 a month billed yearly). Yocale Grow, at US$29 a month, does not include clinical notes (prices checked November 19, 2026).

What does CliniQ Pro include?

One price includes scheduling by location, text reminders, patient records, signed notes, before-and-after photos, receipts, the sterilization log, expenses, the accountant summary and automatic mileage.

Does CliniQ Pro work everywhere in Canada?

Yes, in every province and territory, in English and French. Data is hosted in Canada.
