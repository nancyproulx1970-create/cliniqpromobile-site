The receipt is sent before your coat is back on.

No more receipt book or invoices to redo at night: the receipt is prepared at the patient's home, in a few taps.

Numbered invoices, with no gaps

Each invoice gets its number, with no gaps in the sequence. Once issued, a receipt can no longer be changed.

The PDF receipt, the way the patient prefers

The receipt can be printed or sent to the patient by email or text message. Your income is already counted for the end of the year.

Sold a product? Add it

A cream or another product is added to the invoice after the visit.

Sales taxes, based on your situation

If you are not registered for sales taxes, no tax is added to your invoices, and the app warns you when your taxable sales get close to the threshold where registration becomes mandatory.

The app is in French for now; the English version is in preparation.

Frequently asked questions

Can I bill for a product, like a cream?

Yes. You add it to the invoice after the visit.

What if I am not registered for sales taxes?

The app takes it into account: no tax is added to your invoices. It warns you when your taxable sales get close to the threshold where registration becomes mandatory.
