Switching to CliniQ Pro from other software: import your patients

Short answer: to switch from other software or an Excel sheet to CliniQ Pro, export your patient list as a CSV or Excel (.xlsx) file, then import it in one go. The app checks the file before writing anything, and the import can be undone.

1. Export your patient list

In your current software, look for “Export” or “Download client list”, in CSV or Excel format. An Excel sheet you keep yourself works too. An old Excel file (.xls) must first be saved as .xlsx or CSV.

2. Import it into CliniQ Pro

Choose the file. CliniQ Pro recognizes common columns:

  • file number, first and last name (or the full name in one column);
  • date of birth, phone, second phone and email;
  • address, in separate columns or in one, and room;
  • responsible person: name, relationship, phone and email.

3. Check before importing

A check shows what will be imported, without writing anything. Duplicates and rows to review are flagged. You import only when everything is right, and you can undo the import: the records are then archived, never deleted.

4. Your old notes

The import moves your patient list, not your note history. Keep your old records according to your college's retention rules. If needed, you can attach a PDF of an old record to the patient's file in CliniQ Pro as a document received.

5. You can leave whenever you want

Your patient list can be exported as CSV at any time. Your data goes with you, both ways.

Frequently asked questions

Can I import my patients from other software?

Yes. Export your list as CSV or Excel (.xlsx), then import it into CliniQ Pro in one go. A check shows everything before the import.

Are my old notes imported?

No. The import moves your patient list. Keep your old records according to your college's rules, and attach a PDF to the patient's file if needed.

Can I undo an import?

Yes. The imported records are then archived, never deleted.
