Software and app for massage therapists

For mobile and clinic massage therapy.

At clients' homes, at work sites, in residences or at your clinic: your schedule, session notes, insurance receipts, mileage and your accountant's paperwork, in one app.

Short answer: CliniQ Pro is software and an app for self-employed massage therapists in Canada, mobile or in clinic. Scheduling, session notes, receipts for insurance, expenses, automatic mileage and an accountant summary in one app, in English or French. Data is hosted in Canada. $44.95 a month or $449.50 a year, after a 14-day free trial with no credit card. Taxes and massage therapy in 2026.

Receipts ready for insurance

Numbered invoice and PDF receipt, handed over on the spot, by email or by text. Your association or college and your registration number appear on it: you enter them once in your profile.

A session note in seconds

A note template built for massage therapy: what the client reports, areas treated, techniques used, reaction during treatment and advice. You adapt it to the way you work. Once signed, the note is locked under your name, with the date and time.

Your schedule, at clients' homes or at the clinic

Appointments in a few taps, a text reminder 24 hours before for clients who agree, and "Find a time slot" to fit in a new client by city and length. When you travel, the mileage between visits is calculated for you.

Sales tax and your accountant

Once you are registered, GST and PST, HST or QST are added to your invoices. If not, the app tracks your sales and warns you as you approach the $30,000 threshold. Expenses are filed from a photo of the receipt, and a yearly summary is ready for your accountant.

Frequently asked questions

Is massage therapy still taxable?

It is changing. The November 5, 2026 federal budget proposes to exempt from GST/HST the services of massage therapists who hold the licence required in their province or, where none is required, equivalent qualifications, for services rendered after November 5, 2026. Quebec announced it will harmonize the QST. Until the law is passed, confirm your situation with your accountant, the CRA or your provincial tax authority. In CliniQ Pro, the Taxes apply option is set service by service.

Everything about the massage therapy GST/HST exemption (Budget 2026)

Does my registration number appear on the receipt?

Yes. In your profile, choose your college or association (or "Other" to type it) and enter your number: they appear on every receipt.

I do home visits and also have a clinic. Is that possible?

Yes. In your profile, choose "Both": each client is marked "Seen at the clinic" or "Seen at home", and only home visits count toward mileage.

Can I use CliniQ Pro outside Quebec?

Yes. CliniQ Pro works across Canada, in English and in French. Taxes are added based on your province.

Is CliniQ Pro made for self-employed massage therapists?

Yes. For self-employed massage therapists in a private clinic, at home or doing home visits: scheduling, records, notes, insurance receipts and mileage.
