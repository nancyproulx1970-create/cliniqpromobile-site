In one sentence

CliniQ Pro is the practice software and app for self-employed health professionals in Canada: scheduling, patient records, signed notes, receipts, expenses, mileage and an accountant summary, all in one place.

Description

CliniQ Pro (pronounced “Clinique Pro”) is a mobile practice-management app for self-employed health professionals who work at home visits, in a clinic, or both: nursing, foot care, wound care, rehabilitation, massage therapy, speech therapy, mental health and many more.

It brings together scheduling by work location, patient records with a health assessment, dated consents, signed and locked clinical notes, PDF receipts and invoices with each province's taxes, expenses with a photo of the receipt, and mileage calculated from appointments. At year-end, the professional gives their accountant a summary sorted by income tax return line, and their expenses as an Excel file with the receipt photos.

CliniQ Pro was created by Nancy Proulx, a licensed practical nurse in home foot care, to fit her own days on the road.
