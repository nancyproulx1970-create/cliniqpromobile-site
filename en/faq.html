Account and security

How do I log in to CliniQ Pro?

Open the app, stay on the “Log in” tab, enter your “Email” and your “Password”, then tap “Log in”. If your profile is not filled in yet, the app first takes you to “Profile Settings”.

How do I create my account?

On the login screen, tap the “Sign up” tab. Enter your email, your “Password” and “Confirm password”, then tap “Create my account”. Then open the email you received and click the confirmation link before logging in.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

On the login screen, tap “Forgot your password?”.

1. Enter your account email, then tap “Get a code”.
2. Enter the 6-digit code you received by email, then tap “Continue”. The code is valid for 1 hour; also check your junk mail.
3. Choose a new password, different from the old one: 8 characters or more, with a capital letter, a number and a special character. Enter it twice, then tap “Change my password”.

All your sessions are then closed, on all your devices: log in again with the new password.

How do I sign out?

On the home screen, tap the ☰ menu at the top right, then “Log out” at the very bottom of the list. Confirm with “Log out” in the “Confirm logout” window.

Why does the app ask me to log in again?

For security, the app locks when you reopen it after closing it, after 60 minutes in the background (screen off or another app) and after 15 minutes on without touching the screen. Enter your password and tap “Unlock”: you go back where you were (to the home screen if the app had been closed). After 5 wrong passwords, the app signs you out completely: log in again with your email and password. See also: “Why does the app ask for my password?”.

Can I unlock the app with my fingerprint?

Yes. ☰ menu → “Settings” → “Security”, then turn on “Unlock with fingerprint”. When the app locks, your fingerprint is enough to reopen it. You can always use your password too.

How do I fill in my professional profile?

☰ menu → “Settings” → “My profile”. Fill in your first name, last name, “Professional title”, “Type of practice”, “Licence / member number”, phone, “Professional email” and address, then tap “SAVE PROFILE”. This information appears on your invoices; the address is optional.

How do I add my logo and my signature?

☰ menu → “Settings” → “My profile”. Tap “Add your logo” for the clinic logo.

Signature: in “Professional signature”, tap “Edit”, sign with your finger, then tap “Confirm”. For more space, turn the phone sideways, sign and tap “Confirm” before turning it back upright. Then leave the profile and come back to see the saved signature. You can redo it whenever you want.

When you finalize a note, the app signs it electronically: it is locked under your name, with the date and time, your title and your licence number as they are that day. If you redo your drawn signature later, the new image is the one kept: the app only keeps proof of the one used on the day of the note. The drawn signature appears on printed notes and in the full record copy, for notes finalized after you drew it.

Your signature is kept in a private space: only your account can see it.

How do I set up my sales taxes (GST, QST, HST, PST)?

☰ menu → “Settings” → “My profile”. Higher up, with your address, choose the “Province”: it is used to calculate taxes and is not shown on invoices. Further down, in the “Billing and taxes” section, turn on “Registered for taxes” and enter your “GST number” (the same number is used for HST); in Québec, also enter your “QST number”. Then tap “SAVE PROFILE”. Many health services are tax-exempt: your accountant confirms your situation.

How do I choose my note style (SOAP, etc.)?

☰ menu → “Settings” → “My profile”, “Note preferences” section. In “Note type”, choose “Narrative”, “Structured (SOAP)” or “Simplified hybrid”, then save the profile.

How do I set my schedule settings?

☰ menu → “Settings” → “My schedule”. Enter your “Starting address”, the “Start of day”, the “End-of-day limit” (the time your last visit must be finished; the trip home is not counted), the number of “Maximum patients” per day and whether you are “Available on weekends”. Tap “Save and continue”. Days off are managed in the schedule.

Where can I see the installed app version?

Open the ☰ menu on the home screen: the “Version …” number is shown at the bottom of the menu.

Why does the app ask for my password?

To protect your patients' records if your phone is lost, stolen or lent. The law (in Québec, Law 25) requires you to protect this information, and most clinical record software does the same.

The app locks:
• when you reopen it after closing it;
• after 60 minutes in the background (screen off or another app): so a full visit does not lock it;
• after 15 minutes on without touching the screen or typing.

Enter your password and tap “Unlock”: you go back exactly where you were, and a note in progress stays intact. Without a network, you can unlock with your fingerprint (if turned on) or with your phone's own code. Also keep a code or fingerprint to lock the phone itself.

After 5 wrong passwords, the app signs you out. “Sign out” asks you to confirm, because unsaved text would be lost.

See also: “Why does the app ask me to log in again?”.

What is the “Getting started” tour for?

The first time you open the app, once your profile is filled in, the app guides you through five steps: “My sterilization”, “My vehicle”, “My security”, “My services” and “My patients”. Each step is set up in a few taps, or can be skipped.

If you leave the tour, the app asks “Finish later?”: tap “Continue setup” or “Go to Home”. Everything can be changed later: ☰ menu → “Settings”, “My services and products”, “Finance” → “Mileage” and “Sterilization”.

How do I change the first name shown in “Hello” on the home screen?

The home screen greets you with the first name in your profile. To change it: ☰ menu → “Settings” → “My profile”, correct the “First name” field, then tap “SAVE PROFILE”. If the first name is empty, the home screen shows only “Hello”.

What happens when my free trial ends?

During the last 7 days, the home screen warns you: “Your free trial ends in … days.” (or “Your free trial ends tomorrow.”).

Once the trial has ended, the home screen shows “Your trial has ended. Your records can still be viewed and exported.” If you try to create an appointment, a note or an invoice, the “Trial ended” window opens. You can still view and export your records.

To continue, follow the instructions in the “Trial ended” window.

How do I switch the app to English (or French)?

☰ menu → “Settings” → “Langue / Language”, then choose “English” or “Français”. The app switches language right away.

How do I add a code on top of my password (two-step verification)?

☰ menu → “Settings” → “Security” → “Two-step verification”. Follow the 3 steps:
1. “Install an authenticator app” (Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator, free).
2. “Add CliniQ Pro to the app”: tap “Open my authenticator app”.
3. “Type the code shown”, then tap “Turn on”.

After that, a 6-digit code is asked at each new sign-in. Between patients nothing changes: your fingerprint or password still reopens the app. To remove it, tap “Two-step verification” again and type a code.

Lost phone or deleted app: write to soutien@cliniqpromobile.ca.

Patients

How do I add a new patient?

☰ menu → “Add a patient”. Fill in the first name, last name, date of birth, phone, email and address (street number, street, apt, city, postal code), then tap “Save”. You can also create the patient while booking an appointment with “Add patient”.

How do I find a patient?

☰ menu → “Patients”. Type the name in “Search for a patient...”, then tap the patient to open their “Patient record”. You can also use “Search” in the menu.

How do I change a patient's contact information?

Open the “Patient record”, tap “Edit”, correct the fields, then tap “Save”.

How do I call a patient or get directions to their home?

In the “Patient record”, tap the phone number to call, the email to write, or the address to open directions. On the home screen, each appointment in “MY DAY” also has a phone icon and a directions icon.

What is in the patient record?

The “Patient record” shows the contact information, the status, the assessment and a timeline: visits, visit notes, notes in the record, late entries, documents received and drafts. Tap an item in the timeline to open it.

How do I create or view a patient's health assessment?

From “Appointment details”, tap “+ Assess” if there is none yet, or “📋 View assessment”. From the “Patient record”, tap “Summary”, then “See the summary” or “Edit the summary”. Fill in the history, “Current medication” and “General notes”, then tap “Save”.

How do I update an assessment (yearly review)?

In the “Patient record”, tap “Summary”, then “Edit the summary”. From “Appointment details”, tap “📋 View assessment”, then “Edit assessment”. Make your changes, write the “Reason for the update (optional)” if needed, then tap “Save”. To leave without saving, use the arrow at the top. “To do” reminds you of a health assessment more than a year old.

How do I archive a patient I no longer follow?

In the “Patient record”, tap the status badge at the top (e.g. ACTIVE). In “Patient status”, choose “ARCHIVED” (Will no longer be followed), “INACTIVE” (Follow-up paused, may resume), “ACTIVE” or “DECEASED”. For “ARCHIVED” and “DECEASED”, you are asked to confirm. The record is kept: you can set the patient back to “ACTIVE” at any time.

Can I delete a patient?

No. The app does not delete patient records: change the status instead (“INACTIVE”, “ARCHIVED” or “DECEASED”) from the status badge in the “Patient record”. The record is kept.

How do I import my patient list from other software or Excel?

☰ menu → “Settings” → “Import my patients”. In your old software, export your patient list as an Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file. Choose the file: the app recognizes the columns (first name, last name, date of birth, phone, email, address) and you can correct them. Tap “Check my file”: nothing is added, you see how many patients will be added, which ones are already in CliniQ Pro (skipped) and the lines to look at. Then tap “Import”. The information is copied as is; you correct a patient's details if needed from their record. Wrong file? “Cancel this import” removes the added patients who do not yet have an appointment, a note or an invoice (they are archived, never erased).

How do I export my patient list, and what is “My exports” for?

☰ menu → “Settings” → “Export my patients”, then “Export”. The app creates a CSV file with your patients' names, dates of birth, addresses, phone numbers and emails, including archived patients (no clinical notes), which opens in columns in Excel and can be imported back into CliniQ Pro. Choose where to send it (for example your own email). This is a confidential file: send it only to yourself or to a safe place, and delete it when you no longer need it.

“My exports” (same window) shows the list of your exports: the date, time and number of patients for each one. This record is kept automatically and cannot be changed. It lets you know when your list left the app, justify it if needed (for example to your regulatory college), and check that each export really came from you.

How do I print or share a patient's health assessment?

In the “Patient record”, tap “Summary”, then “See the summary”. In “Patient assessment”, tap “Share or print”. Answer “For whom?”: “The patient”, “Their representative”, “A professional” or “Me (archives)”. For a professional, their name and profession are required. Then tap “Print” or “Share”.

The document carries the word “COPY” and a confidentiality notice. The copy is entered in the record's register. A regular email or text message is not encrypted: handing it over in person or printing it remains the safest.

How do I correct a patient's first or last name?

In the “Patient record”, tap “Edit”, then “Correct name”. Correct the “First name” or the “Last name”, tap “Save the name”, then “Fix it” to confirm.

The record and future receipts carry the new name. Receipts already issued keep the old name.

Schedule

How do I open the schedule?

On the home screen, tap “Open calendar”. You can switch to the “Day”, “Week” or “Month” view.

How do I create an appointment?

In the schedule, tap a free time slot (“Add to calendar”), then “Patient appointment”. In “New Appointment”, choose the patient, “Assessment” or “Follow-up”, the duration, the care, the price and your notes, then tap “Save”.

Why the duration? It reserves the time for the visit in your schedule: the app calculates the end time and refuses another appointment that would overlap that time or one of your unavailable periods.

The care and price are carried over automatically to the invoice for this appointment. You can still change them when you bill.

Products sold (a cream, for example) are not entered in the appointment: you add them after the visit, in the invoice, with “+ Product”.

The app warns me that the travel time is too short. What do I do?

The message means the estimated travel time may make this appointment hard to keep (or that it could not be checked). Choose another time, or tap “Save anyway” to keep it.

How do I find a good time slot automatically?

In the schedule, tap the “Find a time slot” button. Enter the “City” and the “Visit duration”, then tap “View suggestions”. Tap one of the “Suggested time slots”: “New Appointment” opens at that date and time.

The app first suggests days you have already started, to fill them, then a free day, starting from the end of the week. See also: “How does the app choose the suggested time?”.

How do I see the details of an appointment?

Tap the appointment in the schedule or in “MY DAY” on the home screen. The “Appointment details” screen opens with the actions: clinical note, photos, assessment, billing. The “?” at the top explains the actions.

How do I move an appointment?

In “Appointment details”, tap “Edit”. In “Reschedule appointment”, choose the new date and time, then “Confirm”. This is only possible for a scheduled appointment with no note, photo, pouch, invoice or receipt.

How do I cancel an appointment?

In “Appointment details”, tap “Cancel”. Choose the reason (Cancelled by the patient, Hospitalized patient, Refusal of care, No one answers, Unnecessary trip or Other with “Specify the reason”), then “✓ Confirm cancellation”. The reason is kept to justify the trip.

How do I delete an appointment created by mistake?

In “Appointment details”, tap “Delete”, then confirm: this is permanent. If a note, photos, pouches, an invoice or a receipt are linked to it, the app shows “This appointment can no longer be deleted”: use “Cancel” instead.

The patient is not there when I arrive. What do I do?

On the home screen, under the appointment in “MY DAY”, check “Patient absent”: your trip is counted even without a visit. If you then move this appointment to another date (“Edit”), today's trip stays counted: the app keeps a “Patient absent” record at the original date and time, and the new appointment starts over with no trip.

How do I block off time (vacation, training)?

In the schedule, tap a free time slot, then “Unavailability”. Choose “All day”, “A few hours” or “Several days”, the dates and times, a “Reason (optional)”, then “Save unavailability”. No appointment can then be added during that time.

How do I edit or delete an unavailable period?

In the schedule, tap the “Unavailable” block (or the day banner “Tap to edit or delete”). Make your changes and save, or tap “Delete unavailability” and confirm.

How do I add a business activity (no patient)?

In the schedule, tap a free time slot, then “Business activity”. Choose the “Activity type” and the duration, add the “Address”, “Details” or “Notes”, then tap “Save”. No patient data is linked to it.

How do I edit or cancel a business activity?

Tap the activity in the schedule or on the home screen. In “Edit business activity”, tap “Save changes”, or “Cancel activity”: this cannot be undone, the trip is removed and the time slot becomes available again.

How do I confirm the trip for a business activity?

On the home screen, check “Trip completed” on the activity card, only if the trip really took place. In the schedule, a finished activity may also show “Confirm the trip”: answer “Yes, made”, “No” or “Later”.

How do text message appointment reminders work?

You have nothing to do: the reminder goes out on its own 24 hours before each appointment (between 8 a.m. and 9 p.m.), only to patients who agreed.

The agreement is noted once, in “Consents to ask for” (line “Text message reminder”, Yes or No). A patient who was never asked gets no reminder.

The reminder can go to a relative's cell phone (for example a patient's daughter): under “Text message reminder”, tap “Reminder sent to … · Change”.

The text gives your number: the patient calls you directly. To stop all reminders: Settings, “Text message appointment reminders”.

Are statutory holidays shown in the schedule?

Yes. A “Holiday” line appears at the top of the day, and “Holiday” in the Week view. Nothing is blocked: you can book an appointment that day.

Québec statutory holidays are entered in advance, year after year, including Easter. Those for other provinces will be added.

I see my patients at my clinic, how do I set that up?

In “My profile”, under “Where do you see your patients?”, choose “I go to my patients”, “My patients come to me” or “Both”. If your clinic is in your home, check “My clinic is at my address”. With “Both”, each patient has a “Where I see them” choice (At home or At the clinic), also offered when you create a new patient. In a patient's record, the line “Seen at home” or “Seen at the clinic” shows this choice: tap “Change” to edit it. Trips between home and the clinic do not count as business mileage (CRA rule).

How do I save my services (price and length)?

☰ menu → “My services and products” → “My services” (also in ☰ menu → “Settings” → “My services”). Tap “Add a service”, enter the “Service name”, the “Price ($)” and the “Usual length”, then tap “Save”.

When booking, tap “Choose one of my services”: the treatment, price and length fill in on their own. To stop offering a service, turn off “Offered when booking”.

How does the app choose the suggested time?

On a day you have already started, the app places the patient along your route, between your appointments.

On a free day, it first looks at your habits: if you have seen at least 3 patients in that city in the last 6 months, it suggests the time you usually go there. Otherwise, it looks at the shape of your day: if you finish somewhere other than your starting point (for example where you sterilize), a patient near the end is placed at the end of the day; if you come back to your starting point, a patient far away (20 minutes or more) is placed in the middle of the day. A patient nearby is placed at the start of the day.

The more you use the app, the more the suggestions match the way you work. You are always free to choose another time.

I work some days in a long-term care home or a clinic: how do I keep the app from booking patients then?

☰ menu → “Settings” → “My schedule”: uncheck that day in your working days, then save. “Find a time slot” will never suggest that day again, but you can still add an appointment yourself that day, as you wish. For a single date (vacation, training), use an unavailability in the schedule instead.

Clinical notes

How do I write a visit note?

Open the appointment, then tap “Note”. Write your observations in the “Write clinical note” area. Visit notes are always created from the appointment.

What is “Insert my template” for?

Your template is the skeleton of your note: the headings and sentences that come back at every visit. You write it only once; then “Insert my template” places it in any patient's note and you only fill in what changes.

To create it: in a visit note, tap “Insert my template”. The first time, the app suggests a template based on your profession; adjust it, then tap “Save”. After that, it goes in with one tap and you make corrections directly in the note. To change the template itself, tap “Change my template” in the message that appears after inserting it. Do not put any patient name or information in it. The “…” are to be filled in: the app warns you if any are left when you finalize.

Example template:
Reason for visit: …
Observations: …
Care provided: …
Person's response: …
Recommendations: …
Follow-up: …

Is my note saved if I leave?

Yes, the note stays as a draft and is saved automatically while you write. If the app shows “Save not confirmed”, keep the screen open and try again before leaving.

How do I go back to a note I started?

In “To do” (home screen), tap “Resume note”. You can also open it from the timeline in the “Patient record”, where it appears as a draft.

I removed a draft by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes. Right away, tap “Cancel” in the message at the bottom of the screen. Later: in the “Patient record”, tap the “Add” button at the top right, then “Deleted items”, then “Restore” next to the draft. Only an empty draft can be removed.

Can I dictate my note instead of typing it?

Yes, use the microphone on your phone's keyboard to dictate into the note's text area.

How do I finalize and sign a note?

Tap “Finalize”. In “Confirm finalization?”, check “I have reread and checked this text.”: the “Finalize and sign” button stays greyed out until the box is checked. Tap it; the note then shows “FINALIZED”.

Can I change a note that is already finalized?

No. A finalized and signed note can no longer be changed directly. Any correction must be added as a late entry. See also: “Can anyone change a finalized note?”.

How do I add a late entry?

In the “Patient record”, tap the “Add” button at the top right, then “Late entry”. In “For which appointment?”, choose the past appointment concerned, then write the note. It is used to complete a note for a visit already done.

How do I record information outside a visit (call, hospital stay)?

In the “Patient record”, tap the “Add” button at the top right, then “Note in the record”. Choose the “Note category”, describe the event, then tap “Save” (draft) or “Finalize”. For care given during a visit, use the clinical note instead.

How do I take care photos?

In “Appointment details”, tap “Photos” (or “Manage” in the clinical note). In “Visit photos”, tap the camera icon. Wait for “Photo saved ✓”; “Photo being processed…” is shown while it uploads.

How do I tag a photo (before/after, side)?

Tap the photo in “Visit photos”. Choose the “Timing” (Before, After, Instrument, Document), the “Side” (Left, Right, Both sides) and a “Label”, then tap “Save”.

How do I delete a photo?

Tap the photo in “Visit photos”, then the delete button, and confirm “Delete”. The photo is removed from the visit, but it stays in the record for traceability: you can see it in the “Patient record”: the “Add” button at the top right, then “Deleted items”. A photo linked to a finalized note can no longer be removed.

The app reminds me of a billed visit with no note. What do I do?

In “To do”, tap “Write” to write the observation. If no note is needed, tap “Don't remind me again for this visit”.

The app mentions consent when I want to take photos. What do I do?

When you tap “Photos” in “Appointment details”, the app checks the patient's consent to clinical photos.

• “Photo consent not recorded”: ask for the patient's agreement, then tap “Record consent”. “Continue without recording” opens the photos anyway.
• “Photos declined”: the patient declined or withdrew consent. No photo can be added.

I want to try a feature: where should I do my tests?

Create a test patient, for example “Test Patient”, and do all your tests in their record. Never test in a real patient's record: a finalized note is signed and can never be erased, just like a paper record. When you no longer need it, set the test patient to “ARCHIVED”.

Billing

How do I bill an appointment?

In “Appointment details”, tap “Bill”. In “Appointment Billing”, add your lines with “+ Treatment” or “+ Product”, choose the payment method, add a note for the invoice if needed, then tap “Confirm”. In “Issue the receipt?”, check the summary and tap “Issue”. The receipt or invoice then opens.

Which payment methods can I choose?

“Not paid”, “Cash”, “Debit card”, “Credit card”, “Bank transfer”, “Other”, “Cheque” or “Complimentary”. With “Not paid”, an invoice is issued and remains to be collected; with a paid method, a receipt is issued.

How do I give a visit for free (complimentary)?

In “Appointment Billing”, choose the payment method “Complimentary”, then “Confirm” and “Issue”. The balance is $0. An amount of $0 is only accepted with “Complimentary”.

How do I record payment for an invoice paid later?

In “Appointment details”, tap “Collect payment”, or go to Finance → “Receivable” tile → “Unpaid invoices” → “Record payment ›”. Choose the method (Cash, Debit card, Credit card, Transfer or Cheque): “Invoice paid” confirms it.

Where can I look at an invoice or receipt again?

In “Appointment details”, tap “View invoice” (or “View receipt” if it is paid). You can also use “Search” in the ☰ menu and tap the invoice found.

How do I print or send a receipt to the patient?

On the receipt screen, tap “PRINT” to print the PDF, or “SEND” to share it (email, text message…). By email, the subject is “Your receipt” (or “Your invoice”).

Can I change or cancel an invoice already issued?

No, a finalized invoice cannot be changed in the app. A correction is made by replacement: the original is kept with “CANCELLED — replaced by #…” and the new one shows “Replaces #…”. This replacement does not have a button in the app yet. For a declined payment or the wrong payment method, tap “Correct the payment” under the amount paid instead (see also: “The payment didn't go through or I chose the wrong payment method: what do I do?”).

How does invoice numbering work?

Before your first invoice, enter the “Invoice starting number” in “Settings” → “My profile”. After that, it is locked: numbering follows your last invoice. The profile shows “Last invoice” and “Next invoice”.

How are taxes calculated on my invoices?

Taxes are calculated when the invoice is confirmed, based on your tax profile (“Registered for taxes” and “Province” in “Settings” → “My profile”).

What are invoices marked “Copy” from old software?

If your old invoices were brought over from other software when you started with CliniQ Pro, their PDF carries the note “Copy: document originally issued in …”. They are exact copies, not new invoices: they count in your income and your quarterly reports. If you never had invoices brought over, you will not see any.

How do I save my products so I don't have to retype them on every invoice?

☰ menu → “My services and products” → “My products” → “Add a product”. Write the “Product name” and the “Price before taxes ($)”, then tap “Save”. To change a name or price, tap the product. To stop offering it, turn off “Available at billing”: receipts already issued do not change.

When billing: in “Appointment Billing”, tap “+ Product” and choose the product from the list. For a product that is not there, tap “Other product (enter by hand)”. “Manage my products” opens your list.

Taxes on products are calculated at billing based on your tax profile.

The payment didn't go through or I chose the wrong payment method: what do I do?

In “Appointment details”, tap “Correct the payment”, under the amount paid (the link only appears when the appointment has a paid receipt). Choose:
• “The payment didn't go through”: the receipt becomes an unpaid invoice, to collect later;
• “Change the payment method”: choose the right method.
Confirm with “Fix it”.

The receipt is never changed: it stays in the record, cancelled and replaced by a new one.

Financial Centre

Where can I see my income for the month and the year?

☰ menu → “Finance”. The “Financial Centre” shows “Income this month” (“This month” tile) and “Income year to date” (“This year” tile). Tap a tile to open the details: “Income this month” or “Income year to date”.

How do I get an income report for a month or a year?

☰ menu → “Finance”, then tap the “This month” or “This year” tile. Use the arrows to change the month or year. The list shows the period's invoices, “Paid” or “Unpaid”. Tap “Generate the report (PDF)” to create the report.

How do I prepare my quarterly sales tax return?

☰ menu → “Finance” → “Sales tax report” (shown only if “Registered for taxes” is turned on in your profile). Choose the quarter (or “Full year”) and the year. You see the “Income collected (taxes included)”, the “Sales before taxes” and the taxes collected for your province (GST and QST in Québec; HST, or GST and PST, elsewhere). Tap “Generate the report (PDF)” to create the report. Check the amounts with your accountant.

Where can I see the amounts still owed to me?

Financial Centre → “Receivable” tile. The “Unpaid invoices” list shows the “Amount receivable”; tap “Record payment ›” on an invoice to record its payment.

How do I send my yearly totals to my accountant?

☰ menu → “Finance” → “Send to my accountant” button. Choose the year, check the totals, then tap “Send to my accountant”: a PDF is prepared and you choose how to send it (email, text message, Drive…). It contains only totals, no patient names. Your receipts, invoices and odometer photos stay in the app if you are asked for them.

Do the amounts follow the lines of my income tax return?

Yes. The summary follows the lines of the federal T2125 form (gross income 8299, advertising 8521, bank charges 8710, licences, dues and training (line 8760), office expenses 8810, supplies 8811, telephone and Internet 9220, other expenses 9270, vehicle 9281, total 9368, net income 9369, business-use-of-home 9945). In Québec, form TP-80 uses the same headings. For the vehicle, the work share is calculated with your odometer readings (work km ÷ total km); parking counts at 100%. For expenses that are also used at home (home office, for example), you enter the business-use share in the “My business-use shares” card; until it is entered, these expenses stay under “To allocate (not counted yet)”. Confirm the right percentage with your accountant. Your accountant always confirms the classification.

Why doesn't my summary for the accountant show any GST or QST?

If the “Registered for taxes” option is not checked in your profile, you do not collect GST or QST on your invoices. The summary says so: “Sales tax registration: No” and “Sales taxes collected: None”.

In that case, the taxes you pay on your purchases cannot be recovered: they are included in the amount of your expenses, which remain deductible.

Many health services are tax-exempt, but products sold (and some services) are taxable. When your taxable sales over the last 12 months get close to $30,000, the summary warns you: above that, you must register for sales taxes (in Québec: Revenu Québec, for GST and QST; elsewhere: Canada Revenue Agency), then enter your numbers in your profile and check “Registered for taxes”.

Your accountant confirms your situation.

How do I enter my business-use share for an expense category?

What does “To allocate (not counted yet)” mean on my accountant's sheet?

These are expenses that are also used at home and whose business-use share has not been entered yet. They are kept, but they do not count yet in your total expenses.

What does “business use to be determined” mean on my accountant's sheet?

The app does not have what it needs to calculate your vehicle's work share. Your vehicle expenses are kept, but their business-use share is not calculated yet.

• “enter your odometer readings”: you need at least two odometer readings entered in the year, one at the start and one later. ☰ menu → “Finance” → “Mileage” → “Add odometer”.

Why are there two “Work km” lines on my accountant's sheet?

“Work km (this period)” counts your work kilometres between your first and last odometer reading of the year: this is what is used to calculate the vehicle's business use.

“Work km (full year)” counts all your work kilometres, from January 1 to December 31.

The two numbers are the same when all your work trips fall between your two odometer readings.

What does “expense note” next to a business-use share mean?

It is the note you wrote when entering the business-use share for this expense, to say what is excluded (for example: snacks). It is copied as is for your accountant.

How do I start tracking mileage?

A photo is recommended as proof, but not required (see also: “Is it mandatory to take a photo of the odometer?”).

Why does the app ask for the odometer?

The odometer value gives the vehicle's total mileage. Compared with your work kilometres, it is used to calculate the percentage of business use so you can deduct your expenses on your income taxes. The photo is kept as proof; an end-of-month photo also serves as the start of the next month.

I'm using another car, or mine is back from the garage: when do I take the odometer?

No need for a photo before each patient: the kilometres for your visits are calculated from the addresses. The odometer is only used to know each vehicle's total mileage.

• Your car is back from the garage: nothing more to do; keep taking the end-of-month photo.
• You are using another car (borrowed, rented or new): add it as a vehicle (☰ menu → “Finance” → “Mileage”, ⋮ on the vehicle's card, then “Add another vehicle”), take a photo of its odometer when you start using it, then another when you return it or stop using it. A photo of the dashboard is enough, even if the owner is not there. If you don't add it, the kilometres for these visits will count for your own car: its business use will look higher than it really is.
• To find out which expenses for a borrowed or rented car you can deduct, check with your accountant.

The app does not yet remind you of these start and return photos: only the end-of-month reminder exists. For another car, this reminder only comes in a month when you took a photo of its odometer. If you keep it for several months, take its photo yourself at the end of each month (“Mileage” → “Add odometer”).

I sold my car (or returned it): how do I stop its reminder?

1. Before you let it go, take a photo of its odometer: these are its last kilometres.
2. ☰ menu → “Finance” → “Mileage”. On your vehicle's card, tap ⋮, then “Vehicle sold or returned”. In “My vehicles”, tap “Sold or returned” next to the car. If you haven't taken the odometer photo yet, tap “Take the photo first”; otherwise, tap “Confirm”.

It no longer gets the end-of-month reminder and is no longer offered. Its history (odometer, expenses) is kept. If you take it back, tap “Put back in use”.

Why isn't the date of my odometer photo read?

In “Save the odometer”, the “Odometer date” field is the day the photo was taken. When the photo file contains its date, the app uses it and shows “Date taken from the photo. How do I remove an odometer reading?

In “My odometer readings”, tap ⋮, then “Remove this odometer reading”. Choose the reason and tap “Remove”. The reading stays in the history but no longer counts in your kilometres; this is not possible if it is part of a finalized report.

Where can I see my kilometres for the month?

Why don't today's kilometres show up yet?

That's normal: the app calculates a day's kilometres once the day is over, to take all your changes into account (appointments added, moved, patient absent). The next day, when you open the app, the previous day's trips are calculated on their own: leaving home, each patient in order, then the return. A trip to an absent patient is counted: it shows “Cancelled in the schedule” with “Trip made”. You can then check them in “Mileage”: tap the day.

Usually you don't need it: mileage is calculated on its own. “Force a recalculation”, at the bottom of the “Mileage” page, is only for after an outage. Tap it, read the message, then tap “Recalculate”: the app redoes your days from the date given in the message. Days already confirmed and imported kilometres do not change.

How do I check a day of travel?

Do my trips for gas, the bank or the post office count in my kilometres?

Privacy and rules

Is any data permanently deleted?

Patient records are never deleted: their status is changed. Removed expenses and odometer readings stay in the history, cancelled invoices are kept, removed photos stay in the record and removed drafts can be restored. Only exception: an appointment with no note, photo, pouch, invoice or receipt can be permanently deleted.

Can anyone change a finalized note?

No. A finalized and signed note can no longer be changed; corrections are added as late entries, which keeps a trace of the original. See also: “How do I add a late entry?”.

Where are my data and my patients' data kept?

In Canada. Patient details, notes, appointments, invoices, photos and documents are hosted by Supabase, in the Canada Central region (Montréal). They are protected while being sent (encrypted connection) and on the servers.

Each professional sees only their own data: the server refuses any reading of another account's data. Care photos are in a private space and only open with a temporary link.

Which outside services does the app use, and what do they receive?

• Google (route calculation: mileage, travel time between two appointments and “Find a time slot”): receives only the departure and arrival addresses, for example a patient's address, without their name, care or notes. This service may process addresses outside Canada.
• OpenAI, in the United States (automatic reading): if you tap “Read automatically” (odometer) or “Analyze receipt” (expense), it receives that photo to read the numbers. Do not photograph any patient information.
• OpenAI, in the United States (FAQ assistant “Ask my question”): receives the question you type. Never write a patient's name or information in it.
• Sentry, in the United States (app errors): receives only the type of error and the place in the code where it happened. No text you typed, no patient information.
• Twilio, in the United States (text reminders): only if the patient agreed to reminders. It receives their cell number and the reminder text (your first name and title, the day and time of the appointment, your number). Never the patient's name, address, care or notes.

“Improve with AI” is turned off for notes: no clinical note is sent to an artificial intelligence service.

A patient asks for a copy of their record. What do I do?

This is a patient's right everywhere in Canada. Answer in writing within your province's time limit: usually 30 days (in Québec, 30 days, in principle free of charge). Some time limits are shorter: in Manitoba, 72 hours for a patient currently receiving care; others are longer: up to 60 days in Newfoundland and Labrador.

The copy must be readable and, if the patient asks in Québec, in a common electronic format. In the “Patient record”, press and hold the line of the visit or note: check what to provide (the note with its late entries, the visit photos, the invoice or receipt), say who it is for, then tap “Print” or “Share”. Only finalized notes can be copied. Each page carries the word “COPY”, page numbers, your electronic signature and a confidentiality notice. Each copy is entered in the record's register (date, for whom, what was provided). A regular email or text message is not encrypted: handing it over in person or printing it remains the safest.

A correction requested by the patient is added to the record; a signed note is never erased.

Sterilization

How do I keep my sterilization log?

☰ menu → “Sterilization”. The first time, enter your sterilizer (any brand), your next load and pouch numbers (to continue your paper log) and your instruments with their code.

For each cycle: tap “New load”, tap an instrument for each pouch, then mark “Pass” or “Fail” for temperature and time, the external indicator and the internal indicator. Tap “Save the load”. The app gives the load number and the codes to write on the pouches (e.g. 94-MC-0640).

A saved cycle cannot be changed or erased. To correct it, open the load in “Recent loads” and tap “Add an annotation”.

How do I record the instruments used for a patient?

In the patient's visit, on the “Instrument traceability” card, tap “Record the pouches used” and check the pouches opened (search by number, e.g. 0640). Only pouches from a load that passed are offered. A pouch recorded by mistake can be removed from the visit within 24 hours.

What do I do if a spore test is positive?

☰ menu → “Sterilization”, in “Spore test”, tap “Positive” next to the test. The app recalls all loads from this sterilizer since the last negative test, blocks their unused pouches and shows the list of patients visited with these instruments, to assess and notify if needed according to your regulatory college's rules.

The sterilizer stays “to check”: do a new test (empty cycle), record it and enter its negative result before sterilizing instruments.

Test frequency: the Canadian standard CSA Z314 calls for a test every day of use; the app reminds you according to your setting (gear icon).

How do I add pouches sterilized before I started using the app?

☰ menu → “Sterilization” → “Add already sterilized pouches”. Write each code as it appears on the pouch (e.g. 91-EX-0650) or tap “Photograph pouches”.

You can photograph several pouches at once: place them side by side, codes clearly visible, in good light. Then check each code the app shows: it must be exactly the one written on the pouch. Remove (✕) a misread code and write it by hand.

For each load, choose its date from your paper log and confirm that its indicators passed. These pouches are marked “recorded afterwards”; your paper log remains the proof for these loads.

Can I read my pouches with the camera during the visit?

Yes, for printed labels. Tap “Pouches” (Appointment details) or “Record the pouches used” (visit), then “Read my pouches with the camera”.

The camera only reads printed labels (from the sterilizer or a printer). A handwritten code, with a felt-tip marker for example, is not read: type its last digits in “Search for a code”, then check the pouch.

The photo is sent to the CliniQ Pro server to read the square code, then discarded: it is not kept and is not given to any artificial intelligence service. Without a connection, the phone tries to read the code itself.

How do I attach the sterilizer's printout?

After “Save the load”, tap “Photograph the printout”. You can also do it later: ☰ menu → “Sterilization” → tap the load in “Recent loads” → “Photograph the printout”. The photo stays attached to the cycle; it cannot be replaced or erased.

Can my pouches be written into the note?

Yes. “Insert my template” fills the “Traceability:” line with the codes recorded for the visit. If any are missing, before you finalize the app asks “Add traceability?”: tap “Add”. Always reread the note before signing it.

How do I record a spore test and its result?

☰ menu → “Sterilization”, “Spore test” section. Tap “Record a spore test” on the day the sample goes into the sterilizer, check the date, tap “Record the test”, then “Record”. Once recorded, a test can no longer be changed.

When the lab answers, tap “Negative” or “Positive” next to the pending test and confirm. You can attach the photo of the lab certificate to the test.

The test was already done and its result is negative? Tap “Record a past test” and enter the date written on the certificate: it will be marked “recorded afterwards”.

“See all my tests” shows the full list.

I recorded a spore test by mistake. How do I correct it?

A recorded test cannot be erased, but you can cross it out. ☰ menu → “Sterilization” → “See all my tests”. Under the test, tap “This test was recorded in error”, write the reason (for example: wrong date), then tap “Cross out this test”.

The test stays visible, crossed out, and no longer counts as the latest test. This cannot be undone. A positive test cannot be crossed out.

A certificate attached to the wrong test is corrected in the same place: tap “Remove” next to the photo, then “Attach it to another test” or “Just remove it”.

Can I photograph several pouches at the same time?

Yes. Place 2 to 5 pouches side by side, labels clearly visible, and take a single photo: the app lists each printed label it finds (“Label 1”, “Label 2”…).

For a good reading: pouches flat, good light, clean and dry labels, no glare. Wait for the reading to finish before taking another photo.

When a label's small square code is read, the sterilization date and number are certain. When it is not read, the app says so and marks what it read “confirm on the pouch”: then check on the pouch.

The camera reads printed labels only. A handwritten code is searched for by its digits.
