Privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This English version is a translation provided for convenience. In case of any discrepancy, the French version prevails.

1. Who are we?

CliniQ Pro is a mobile practice management application for self-employed health professionals who work in patients’ homes, in a clinic or both (hereinafter “the Application”).

It is operated by the company 18325006 Canada Inc.

2. Purpose

This policy describes the practices relating to the personal information and confidential data for which CliniQ Pro acts as the person in charge and as a service provider under the Act respecting the Protection of Personal Information in the Private Sector (APPIPS, in French LPRPSP) or any other applicable personal information protection law.

3. The role of CliniQ Pro

CliniQ Pro acts in two different capacities. This is an important distinction that determines whom you should contact.

For the personal information and confidential data of your patients, CliniQ Pro is not responsible for the collection, use or communication to third parties that You carry out to provide your services as a health professional. The patient records, care records, notes, assessments, photos and appointments that you enter in the Application are processed under your responsibility.

You are the person in charge of the protection of personal information within the meaning of section 3.1 of the APPIPS or any other applicable personal information protection law, including those that specifically protect health and social services information, namely, in Quebec, the Act respecting health and social services information (AHSSI, in French LRSSS). CliniQ Pro acts as a service provider within the meaning of section 18.3 of the APPIPS, following your instructions and to make the Application available to you under the subscription You have taken out.

You are also generally responsible for the confidentiality of your patients’ data under the confidentiality and professional secrecy rules specific to the professional order to which you belong.

For the data that concerns you directly as a health professional, CliniQ Pro is responsible. This includes your account, your monthly or annual subscription, your billing, your exchanges with our customer service or your support requests, the security of the platform and the detection of errors.

4. Scope

This policy applies to any health professional (hereinafter “You” or “the User”) who uses the Application by creating an account or signing in to an existing account in the Application, available on iPhone or Android, and who uses the various features offered by the Application.

5. Data You provide to CliniQ Pro

5.1 Data collected by CliniQ Pro to make the Application available

When you download our Application on iPhone or Android to use it in your work as a health professional, You provide us in particular with the following data, which CliniQ Pro processes on its own behalf:

Data about your organization:

  • legal name of your organization,

  • address,

  • email,

  • telephone,

  • business number (if applicable).

Data about the authorized representatives of your organization:

  • last name,

  • first name,

  • position,

  • email address,

  • telephone number.

Billing details of the organization:

  • billing address,

  • billing email,

  • transaction or billing information,

  • customer number.

Data provided to answer your support requests:

  • content of your exchanges with our support team,

  • technical elements you send us to identify a specific problem, such as a screenshot.

Data provided to answer your requests for information about the Application:

  • name,

  • email address,

  • profession,

  • province,

  • telephone.

Data provided to ensure the security of the Application and to detect and correct incidents and errors:

  • technical and diagnostic information about the device and the browser,

  • IP address, connection and activity logs,

  • logs of changes to and consultations of records.

Data collected automatically about devices and their use is used only within the above framework, to detect and diagnose technical errors.

Registration to use the Application is designed for the collection and processing of information of a professional nature, including business contact information and other information relating to the performance of duties within a business or organization, which is, to the extent provided by the APPIPS, excluded from the application of some of its provisions.

The User is solely responsible for the nature and content of the information transmitted through the Application and agrees to transmit only information that is necessary and directly related to their professional activity. In particular, the User must ensure that no personal information unrelated to this context is communicated, unless legally authorized to do so and in compliance with all applicable obligations.

The Application does not use your phone’s GPS location. CliniQ Pro does not sell or rent your data. No advertising is shown in the Application.

5.2 Exclusions

Excluded from CliniQ Pro’s collection on its own behalf are the health and social services information and confidential data of your patients:

  • identification information,

  • contact information (email, telephone, mailing address),

  • appointments,

  • medical record and health assessment,

  • care notes,

  • care photos,

  • consents.

Excluded from CliniQ Pro’s collection on its own behalf are the information and data concerning your accounting and your mileage:

  • invoices,

  • payments received,

  • expenses and receipt photos,

  • vehicle and odometer photos,

  • tax numbers.

CliniQ Pro does not collect or use this health information and these data for its own purposes. CliniQ Pro processes them exclusively on behalf of the User and in accordance with the User’s instructions, to the extent necessary to provide the Application and its features. This may include their hosting and their transmission to the service providers identified below when needed to use a feature activated by the User.

As the person in charge of your patients’ data (health and social services information), it is your responsibility to retain it in accordance with the APPIPS or any other applicable personal information protection law, and with any regulatory retention periods or those set by your professional order.

Your patients exercise their rights of access, rectification or withdrawal of consent by contacting You directly, as the person in charge of the data and personal information that concerns them.

As the person in charge of the collection and use of your patients’ personal information, for the purposes communicated when they came into your care, You must ensure that communicating their personal information to CliniQ Pro to perform the service contract, namely making the Application available, is lawful under the applicable personal information protection laws.

The User also remains responsible for ensuring that the patients’ personal information communicated to CliniQ Pro was collected and communicated in accordance with applicable laws.

CliniQ Pro does not require or request the communication of patients’ personal information other than what is needed to use the Application and its features.

Should unrequired, excessive or irrelevant personal information be transmitted to CliniQ Pro, the User acknowledges that this transmission is made under the User’s sole responsibility.

6. How your data is collected

  • Directly from you: when You provide your data by registering in the Application and by completing our form available on our website www.cliniqpromobile.ca;

  • Through third parties: from our payment service provider (Stripe), which sends us the details of your subscription and of the payments made;

  • Automatically when You visit our website www.cliniqpromobile.ca: anonymized data is collected, such as pages visited, origin of the visit, language and type of device, without IP address or identifier.

7. Purposes of collection by CliniQ Pro

The data and personal information communicated will be processed and used in accordance with the APPIPS or any other applicable personal information protection law, with the consent You have given and with the other conditions and exceptions provided by applicable law.

We may therefore use the data and personal information provided in the following circumstances and for the following reasons:

  • creating and managing your professional account;

  • billing your subscription and keeping our accounts;

  • answering your support requests;

  • answering your demonstration and contact requests;

  • ensuring the security of the platform and detecting incidents;

  • communicating with you about your file;

  • meeting our legal, accounting and reporting obligations.

We will use the data and personal information only to achieve the purposes for which they were collected and for any other purpose authorized by law.

8. Hosting of data and personal information and communication to third parties

The data and personal information are hosted in Canada, in Montreal, by Supabase (Amazon Web Services infrastructure).

However, to offer you certain features of the Application, we may communicate or give access to your data and to your patients’ personal information, in addition to our authorized internal team, to third parties who help us run certain features of our Application and who provide us with products and services for that purpose:

  • OpenAI: the note-writing assistant, reading photos of receipts and odometers, and the answers in Help.

  • Google (Google Maps Platform): calculating distances and searching addresses, based on the addresses of your appointments.

  • Twilio: sending appointment reminders by text message to the User’s patient (or to the person responsible), if you turn this feature on (patient’s number, appointment date and time).

  • Sentry: the error reports described above.

  • Resend: sending appointment reminders by email to patients and the free-trial emails for the Application.

  • Environment and Climate Change Canada: the weather on the home screen, based on your city.

  • Stripe: processing payments for subscriptions to the Application. The information needed to process the payment is sent directly to this provider and is then processed in accordance with Stripe’s applicable privacy practices and policies. CliniQ Pro does not have access to, and does not keep, the full data of your payment card.

Since these third parties may have access to or process data and personal information as part of the services they provide to us, we limit the data and information we give them to what is reasonably necessary for them to perform their functions.

To this end, we require these providers to commit, by written contract or confidentiality agreement, to take all reasonable and lawful measures to ensure the confidentiality and protection of this information, in accordance with this privacy policy.

Data and personal information that may be communicated, processed or kept in another jurisdiction may be subject to the applicable laws of that jurisdiction.

When required by law, we carry out a privacy impact assessment beforehand (analysis of the security issues and of the applicable law) and put in place the appropriate contractual and security measures to ensure adequate protection of personal information before it is communicated outside Quebec.

Your data and personal information may be communicated to government, regulatory or law enforcement bodies when we are required to communicate this information or if we believe in good faith that this disclosure is necessary to comply with applicable laws.

9. Retention period

We will keep your data and personal information to fulfil the purposes for which they were communicated to us and for any compatible or related authorized purpose.

As long as You use the Application (active account), your data and personal information are kept and remain available.

When You stop using the Application, you have ninety (90) days to export your data and your patients’ personal information. The Application will be set to read-only during this period.

After this period, they are permanently and securely destroyed from our active systems, subject to any legal or regulatory retention periods. They may, however, remain temporarily in backup copies (7 days) until they are deleted or overwritten according to the retention cycle that applies to backups, without being restored to production except when required for continuity, security or recovery after an incident.

In accordance with section 5.2, as the person in charge of your patients’ data, it is your responsibility to retain it in accordance with the APPIPS or any other applicable personal information and health information protection law, and with any regulatory retention periods or those set by your professional order.

10. Security measures

CliniQ Pro recognizes that information security is an integral part of the confidentiality of data and personal information.

CliniQ Pro implements reasonable security measures to protect data and personal information in accordance with applicable protection legislation and, in particular, applies administrative, technical and physical security measures proportionate to the sensitivity of the data and personal information collected and hosted.

In particular, data is encrypted in transit and at rest. The User’s access to the Application is secured and logged. Daily backups are made.

All CliniQ Pro staff, as well as the third-party providers whose services we may retain and who process data and personal information, in particular to offer you certain features of the Application, are also bound by contract to respect the security and confidentiality of data and personal information.

In the event of a potential or confirmed confidentiality incident affecting the data and personal information communicated by the User, CliniQ Pro notifies the User’s person in charge of the protection of personal information without delay, takes the reasonable measures needed to reduce the risk of harm and to prevent new incidents of the same nature, and works with the User so that the User can meet their applicable legal obligations.

11. Links to other sites

The website www.cliniqpromobile.ca may offer hyperlinks to other sites. Information exchanged on those sites is not subject to this privacy policy but to that of the external site concerned, if there is one. Where applicable, we invite you to read it before communicating your personal information to third parties.

CliniQ Pro cannot be held responsible for the personal information protection policies, procedures and practices of its partners.

12. Your rights

For the personal information for which CliniQ Pro is responsible, You have a right of access, of rectification, of withdrawal of consent to the communication or use of your personal information, and of portability, which you can exercise by sending a written and detailed request to CliniQ Pro’s person in charge of the protection of personal information at rprp@cliniqpromobile.ca.

A dedicated page in the Application also lets you close your account.

Reminder: for your patients’ requests, the User remains solely responsible for handling requests for access, rectification, withdrawal of consent, or any other request made under the applicable personal information and health information protection laws. Any person concerned who wishes to exercise such rights must contact the User’s person in charge of the protection of personal information directly.

CliniQ Pro is not required to answer these requests directly, but will reasonably cooperate with the User when its assistance is needed for the User to meet their legal obligations.

If you are not satisfied with how CliniQ Pro handled your request, in particular if you receive no answer or an unsatisfactory answer, you may file an application for the examination of a disagreement with the Commission d’accès à l’information under the APPIPS, or contact the privacy commissioner of your province or, where applicable, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at the federal level.

13. Changes to this policy

We may change the terms of access to the Application and the nature of the data and personal information required.

These changes could lead to changes in our privacy practices and in this privacy policy.

This policy may therefore change. The date of the last update appears at the top of the page.

Any substantial change is reported to you by email or in the Application.

14. Contact us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact our person in charge of the protection of personal information by email at rprp@cliniqpromobile.ca.
