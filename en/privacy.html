Privacy policy

Interim version, November 1, 2026. This policy accurately describes how the app works today. It is being reviewed by our lawyer; the revised version will replace it here and in the app, and you will be notified.

Who we are

CliniQ Pro is a practice management app for self-employed health professionals who see patients at home, in a clinic or both. It is operated by 18325006 Canada Inc. (Quebec).

Person in charge of the protection of personal information

Nancy Proulx, president of 18325006 Canada Inc. For any question, request for access or correction, or complaint: info@cliniqpromobile.ca. We reply within 30 days at the latest.

Two different roles

Your own information, as the professional (name, contact details, business information, expenses, income, mileage): CliniQ Pro is responsible for it.

Your patients' information (records, notes, assessments, care photos, appointments): you, the professional, collect it and are responsible for it, under the rules of your regulatory college and your province. CliniQ Pro hosts and processes it only to provide the service, on your instructions. We do not look at it unless you ask us to help you, and we never use it for any other purpose.

What the app collects, and why

  • Your account: name, email, phone, address, profession, business and tax information. To create your account and produce your invoices and receipts.
  • Your patients: identity, contact details, appointments, record, health assessment, care notes, care photos, consents. To keep your records and your schedule.
  • Your finances: invoices, payments received, expenses and receipt photos, vehicle and odometer photos. For your bookkeeping and mileage.
  • Your support requests: the text you write to us. To answer you.
  • Error reports: phone model, app version and where the error happened, with no record content. To fix problems.

The app does not use your phone's GPS location, shows no advertising and never sells or rents any information.

Where your data is

Data and photos are hosted in Canada, in Montreal, by Supabase (Amazon Web Services infrastructure). Some features use service providers, which process information only to provide that service to us:

  • OpenAI (United States): the note-writing assistant, reading receipt and odometer photos, and Help answers. Do not enter any name or information that identifies a patient.
  • Google (Google Maps Platform): distance calculations and address search, based on your appointment addresses.
  • Twilio (United States): appointment reminders by text message, if you turn this feature on (patient's number, appointment date and time).
  • Sentry (United States): the error reports described above.
  • Environment and Climate Change Canada: the weather on the home screen, based on your city.

Information processed outside Quebec or Canada is handled with equivalent safeguards (agreements with providers, encrypted communications).

How we protect it

  • Encrypted communications between the app and the server.
  • Each professional sees only their own data (server-side access control).
  • App lock and fingerprint; screenshots blocked.
  • A finalized note can no longer be changed; changes are logged.

How long we keep it

Your information is kept as long as your account is open. Your patients' records must be kept for the period required by law and by your regulatory college: this is why an account holding records is not erased immediately when it is closed. Before closing, you can export your data. At the end of the retention period, data is securely destroyed.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or receive a copy of your information, or withdraw your consent, by writing to info@cliniqpromobile.ca. You can close your account in the app (menu, “Privacy and rules”, “Close my account”). If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec or the privacy commissioner of your province. Your patients' requests about their records are addressed to you; we help you answer them.

Privacy incidents

If an incident presents a risk of serious harm, we promptly notify the professionals concerned, the Commission d'accès à l'information and the people affected, and we keep a register of incidents.

This website

This site presents the app. It sets no cookies and does not track anyone. To learn how people find it, it counts visits anonymously: the page viewed, the site the visit came from (for example Google or Facebook), the language and the type of device (phone or computer). No IP address, no identifier, nothing that could identify a person is kept; these figures are stored in Canada. Its only form, “Reserve your spot”, collects your name, email, profession, province and phone type, with your consent, only to write to you about the launch of CliniQ Pro. This information is stored in Canada, never sold or shared, and deleted on request at soutien@cliniqpromobile.ca.

Changes

Any change to this policy is published here and in the app, with its date. If it changes significantly, we will notify you in the app.
