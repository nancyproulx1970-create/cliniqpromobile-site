Which tax applies to my services? Free tool by province and profession

Nurse, RPN, physiotherapist, RMT, kinesiologist: the answer depends on the service you provide and where you practise. Pick both and the tool gives you the rule and the rate.

Short answer: most health care given by a member of a recognized profession is exempt from GST/HST (and QST in Quebec). Kinesiology, cosmetic services and products sold are generally taxable at your province's rate, if you are registered. For massage therapy, an exemption is proposed in the 2026 federal budget.

Free tool: exempt or taxable?

General rule only: your situation depends on your profession, the service and your province. Confirm with your accountant. Nothing is sent: the calculation happens in your browser.

The rule in three lines

Exempt: health care rendered to an individual, within the scope of the profession, by a professional who holds a licence: nursing (including RPNs and LPNs), physiotherapy, occupational therapy, speech-language pathology, psychology, social work, dietetics.

Generally taxable: kinesiology, cosmetic services with no medical purpose, and products you sell (creams, accessories, etc.).

Massage therapy: the November 5, 2026 federal budget proposes to exempt it from GST/HST for services rendered after November 5, 2026. It is a proposed measure, not yet law. The guide on massage therapy and taxes.

The title alone does not decide: the service must be for the protection, maintenance or restoration of health. A report or assessment done for an insurer, a lawyer or an employer can be taxable, even when done by an exempt profession.

Frequently asked questions

Does a self-employed nurse or physiotherapist charge GST/HST?

No, not on exempt health care rendered to a patient within their profession. Products sold and cosmetic services can be taxable.

Is kinesiology taxable?

Yes, generally. If you are registered, you charge GST/HST at your province's rate, plus QST in Quebec. Confirm with your accountant.

Is massage therapy still taxable?

The November 5, 2026 federal budget proposes to exempt it for massage therapists who hold their province's licence (or equivalent qualifications), for services rendered after November 5, 2026. It is a proposed measure: confirm with your accountant until the law is passed.

What is the sales tax rate in my province in 2026?

GST of 5% in Alberta and the territories; HST of 13% in Ontario, 14% in Nova Scotia and 15% in New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador and Prince Edward Island; GST 5% plus a 7% provincial tax in British Columbia and Manitoba, and 6% in Saskatchewan; GST 5% + QST 9.975% in Quebec.
