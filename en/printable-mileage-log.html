Printable mileage log (free, letter size)

Do you drive for work and want to deduct your car expenses? You need a logbook of your trips. Here is a blank one, ready to print.

Short answer: the CRA and Revenu Québec ask you to keep a log of your work trips: the date, the destination, the purpose and the kilometres, plus the odometer reading at the start and end of the year. Pick the number of pages and print this free log on letter-size paper. CliniQ Pro keeps this log automatically from your appointments.

How to fill it in

One line per work trip: the date, where you leave from, where you go, the purpose (for example "home visit" or "buying supplies"), the odometer at the start and at the end, and the kilometres driven. To protect privacy, avoid writing a patient's full name: initials or a file number are enough to find your way around.

At the top of the page, record the odometer at the start and end of the year: that gives your total kilometres, and so the business-use share of your vehicle. Keep your log and receipts for six years.

Which trips to record

Trips to patients' homes, between visits, or to a supplier for work count. Travel between home and your clinic or regular place of work is personal and does not go in the log. Without a logbook, the deduction can be refused in an audit. The mileage deduction guide and calculator.

With CliniQ Pro, the log keeps itself

Trips between your visits are calculated from your schedule, and the day is entered in your mileage log on its own the next morning. You photograph the odometer once a month. Frequently asked questions

What must a mileage log include?

For each work trip: the date, the destination, the purpose and the number of kilometres. Also record the odometer at the start and end of the year. Without a logbook, the deduction can be refused in an audit.

How long should I keep my log?

Six years, with your receipts.

Does the trip between home and my clinic go in the log?

No. Travel between home and your regular place of work is personal. Trips to your patients' homes are work trips.

Can I keep my log in an app?

Yes. What matters is having the date, the destination, the purpose and the kilometres for each trip. CliniQ Pro keeps this log from your appointments and the odometer.
