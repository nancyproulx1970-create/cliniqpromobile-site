Automatic mileage tracking: done by the time you get home

By the time you get home, your mileage is done.

No more logbook in the glove box. Your work trips are calculated from your appointments.

Trips calculated for you

Trips between your visits are calculated from your schedule. At night, one glance to confirm the day, and your mileage log is up to date.

The odometer, once a month

One photo of the odometer a month is enough, not one per trip. It gives the business-use share of your vehicle.

Patient not home? The trip still counts

You check “Patient absent” and the trip is still counted, even without care.

Do your patients come to your clinic?

No driving, so no mileage to keep for them. According to the CRA, the round trip between home and your clinic is personal: it doesn't count. If you do both, only trips to your home-visit patients are added to your log.

Ready for tax time

Your mileage and your vehicle's business-use share are included in the year-end summary sent to your accountant.

The app is available in English and French.

Frequently asked questions

Does the app calculate my mileage?

Yes. Trips between your visits are calculated for you, and you confirm the day at a glance. The odometer is photographed once a month.

My patient is not home when I arrive. Is my trip lost?

No. You check “Patient absent” and the trip is still counted, even without care.
