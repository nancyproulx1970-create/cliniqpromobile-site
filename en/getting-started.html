Getting started: ready in an hour, working the next day

Short answer: take about an hour, ideally on a Sunday, to create your account and follow the app's 7 guided steps: profile, schedule, sterilization, vehicle, security, services and patients. On Monday morning, your day is already in CliniQ Pro.

What to have ready

  • For your profile: your logo if you have one, your business name if any, your professional title, your address, your regulatory body and licence number, your work phone and email, your tax numbers if you're registered, and the starting number for your invoices if you want to continue your old numbering. Also: the places where you see your patients (home visits, clinic, long-term care, retirement home), with their address.
  • For your schedule: your working hours, day by day.
  • For sterilization: if you sterilize instruments, your sterilizer's make and model, its temperature and cycle length, your next lot and pouch numbers, and the list of your instruments.
  • For your vehicle: if you use your vehicle for visits, your odometer reading.
  • For your security: your phone, with an authenticator app. On iPhone, the Passwords app (already installed) works. Otherwise, Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator (both free).
  • For your services: your services and their prices, and your products if you sell any.
  • For your patients: if you already have clients, your list in an Excel or CSV file (last name, first name, cell phone, address, date of birth, and your file number if you have one). Just starting out? Nothing to prepare: you'll add each patient at their first appointment.

Your first hour, in 7 steps

Once your account is created, the app guides you in this order. You can always tap "Later / Next" and come back to it.

  1. Your profile: your identity, regulatory body and licence, your signature drawn with your finger, billing and taxes, your note type, and where you see your patients. Home visits only? Check "At the patient's home". Clinic only? Add your clinic. Both, or also in long-term care or retirement homes? Check "At the patient's home" and add each of your places.
  2. Your schedule: for each day, where you work and your hours.
  3. Sterilization: "Yes, set up my log" opens the log setup directly (sterilizer, numbers, instruments), or "I don't sterilize instruments". To change it later: menu ☰ → Sterilization → gear icon.
  4. My vehicle: add it so your kilometres calculate themselves, or "No, I get around another way".
  5. My security: two-step verification (required) and unlocking with your face or fingerprint.
  6. My services: your services and their prices, and your products.
  7. My patients: import your file (column names don't matter, the app checks everything before importing), or skip this step if you're just starting. Patients who share the same address are grouped: one tap places them in your long-term care or retirement home.

Then enter your first appointments in the schedule. When a patient calls, "Find a time slot" suggests the best times based on your schedule, the place, the length of the visit and real driving time.

What stays in your previous system

Your previous receipts and invoices stay in your previous system: your income in CliniQ Pro starts with your first receipt issued here. Keep your previous notes and paper records as the law requires for your profession; new notes are written in CliniQ Pro. Starting mid-year? Enter your expenses with their real date and a photo of the receipt: they count in your summary for your accountant.

A question? Tap "Contact support" in the app, or see the frequently asked questions.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get started with CliniQ Pro?

About an hour: the app guides you through 7 steps (profile and places, schedule, sterilization, vehicle, security, services, patients), then you enter your first appointments.

Can I import my patients from other software?

Yes, from an Excel or CSV file exported from your previous software. The app lets you match the columns, checks the file before importing, and keeps your file numbers.

Which printer should I bring to print receipts at my patients' homes?

A small portable thermal printer is enough: no ink or cartridges, and it fits easily in a bag. Nancy Proulx, the founder of CliniQ Pro, uses a Phomemo M832 at her patients' homes. She prints in letter size, on thermal paper (not regular paper). Install the Phomemo app on your phone, then tap “Print” on the receipt: it opens directly in the printer's app. No printer? The receipt can also be sent by email or text message.
