Your schedule: home visits, care homes or your clinic

For each day, you choose where you work and your hours. Your calendar follows.

Why it matters

Accurate business mileage. At your clinic, patients come to you: no mileage. On home visits, you do the driving: your mileage is calculated automatically, trip by trip. Accurate numbers for your taxes.

The right patient, on the right day, in the right place. A patient calls? “Find a time slot” knows if they come to your clinic or you go to them, and only suggests days when you are in the right place.

First, your work locations

In Settings, “Where I see my patients” lists all your locations: home visits, your clinic, your care homes and your seniors’ residences. Tap “Add a location”, choose the type, then search by name or address: Google Maps results appear, you check them and they are added. Each place can keep an access note (door code, parking, nursing station). The X removes a place from your list without touching your past appointments.
