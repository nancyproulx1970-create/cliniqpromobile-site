French-English glossary of titles and acronyms for self-employed health professionals

Short answer: an infirmière auxiliaire is an LPN, called an RPN in Ontario. An infirmière autorisée is an RN. GST is TPS, QST is TVQ, HST is TVH, and the CRA is the ARC. A regulatory college is an ordre professionnel. The tables give the rest.

Frequently asked questions

What is an "infirmière auxiliaire" in English?

A Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN). In Ontario, the title is Registered Practical Nurse (RPN).

What does IAA mean?

Infirmière auxiliaire autorisée or infirmier auxiliaire autorisé. It is the French term used outside Quebec for an LPN. In Quebec, the reserved title is "infirmière auxiliaire" or "infirmier auxiliaire".

Does RPN always mean practical nurse?

No. In Ontario, RPN means Registered Practical Nurse. In the four Western provinces, the Yukon and, since December 1, 2025, Nova Scotia, RPN means Registered Psychiatric Nurse, a different profession.

What are TPS, TVQ and TVH in English?

TPS is GST, TVQ is QST and TVH is HST. The ARC is the CRA.
