Frequently asked questions

Can I stop anytime?

Yes, anytime. You keep access until the end of the period already paid (the month or the year), and then it doesn't renew. Periods already started are not refunded. Your records stay available to view and export.

Does the price depend on my profession?

No. The price is the same for every profession and every feature.

Why can't I pay in the app?

You subscribe on our website. It's simpler and keeps the price as low as possible.
