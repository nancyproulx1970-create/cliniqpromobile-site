CliniQ Pro vs CompanyOn: which software for home health professionals?

Your practice. Your business. One tool. One tap, and your accountant has the summary.

Short answer: CompanyOn is Canadian software from British Columbia for mobile care and small clinics, popular with foot care nurses outside Quebec. It offers online booking, a patient portal and insurer billing. CliniQ Pro is software and an app for self-employed health professionals, at home, in clinic or both, in English and French, with expenses, mileage and the accountant's summary in the same app. CompanyOn costs about $5 less per month.

When CompanyOn may suit you better

If you work with a team or an agency, or you want online booking, a patient portal, online payments or direct insurer billing, CompanyOn offers these features, at a slightly lower price.

When CliniQ Pro is made for you

You are self-employed, at home, in a clinic or both, in English or French: nurse, practical nurse, foot care, wound care, rehabilitation, massage therapy or other. You want expenses, mileage and your accountant's summary done in the same app as your records and notes, with no spreadsheet on the side.

Frequently asked questions

Is CliniQ Pro more expensive than CompanyOn?

Slightly. CompanyOn costs $39.99 a month or $431.88 a year for one practitioner, and requires a credit card for the trial. CliniQ Pro costs CA$44.95 a month or CA$449.50 a year, with a 14-day trial and no card. The difference covers expenses read from a photo of the receipt, the year-end summary for your accountant and the sterilization log (prices checked on November 17, 2026).

Is CompanyOn available in French?

The CompanyOn pages we checked do not mention a French version (checked on November 17, 2026). CliniQ Pro is fully in English and French: the app, receipts, emails and support.

Does CompanyOn track mileage?

CompanyOn's website advertises a mileage estimate with its routes. CliniQ Pro calculates trips from your schedule, day by day, with a monthly odometer photo, then carries them into your accountant's summary.

Which one suits foot care nurses?

Both. CompanyOn has an agreement with the Canadian Association of Foot Care Nurses and templates for this work. CliniQ Pro was created by a practical nurse in foot care: photos sorted by foot, a sterilization log and pouches linked to each visit.
